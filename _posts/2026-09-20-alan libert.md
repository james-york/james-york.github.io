## 👨‍🏫 Profile
B.A. in Latin and Greek from New York University, Ph.D. in linguistics from McGill University, lecturer in linguistics at University of Newcastle, Australia since 1994, adjunct professor at Khazar University since 2023, currently living in Spain. My research areas include artificial languages (many books and papers), Turkic languages, word classes (especially adpositions), and onomastics.
